The Rolling Stones have played (I Can’t Get No) Satisfaction thousands of times. They’ve toured the world. Filled stadiums. Decades on stage.

And still – before every big show – they rehearse that song.

They know something many presenters forget: preparation is never optional.

The more you rehearse, the more natural you sound.

The more you rehearse, the more spontaneous you seem.

The more you rehearse, the better your message lands.

Rehearsing doesn’t mean memorising. It means knowing your material so well that you can focus on connecting.

If the Stones rehearse their biggest hit, so can we.
