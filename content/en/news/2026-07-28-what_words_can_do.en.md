In *Sapiens*, Yuval Noah Harari writes that humans dominate the planet not because we’re the strongest or fastest, but because of our unique ability to tell shared stories.

Money, nations, laws – these are all imagined realities. They don’t exist in nature, but they exist in our minds because we collectively believe in them. That shared belief allows us to collaborate in large numbers, unlike any other species.

Words made this possible.

And that’s why communication matters.

Your words have the power to move people – to shift how they think, what they feel and what they do.

You can inspire a team. Sell an idea. Rally support. Calm a room. Spark action.

But only if you treat your words with care.

So, next time you speak, remember the responsibility and the opportunity in every word you use.

Because when used well, words shape the world.
