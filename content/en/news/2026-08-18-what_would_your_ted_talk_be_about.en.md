Imagine this: it’s twenty years from now. You’re on stage, giving a TED Talk that gets a standing ovation and moves people to tears.

What’s the title of that talk?

It’s a question that forces you to think long-term about your impact.

What’s your thing? What do you want to be remembered for?

For me, it comes down to three things:

● Helping others use their voice to make the world a better place\
● Using business as a force for good\
● Being the best dad and husband I can be (even if no one else knows – as long as my wife and son do)

It’s also a useful exercise for presenters. When you’re clear on what you stand for, your message gets stronger. And your audience can feel the difference.

So, what’s your TED Talk about?
