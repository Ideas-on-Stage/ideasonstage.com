And whether you realise it or not, you’re always pitching.

In a meeting? You’re pitching an idea.

Convincing your kids to eat their veggies? That’s a pitch.

Trying to get your friends to agree on where to eat? Still a pitch.

We pitch all the time. And not every pitch will land.

Sometimes, it’s timing. Sometimes, it’s the audience. Sometimes, it’s you. Sometimes, it just doesn’t go your way. That’s part of the deal.

But then, one day, you give the presentation that moves the needle. You connect. You spark action.

That moment only happens because you kept going.

So keep showing up. Keep sharing your ideas. One pitch can change everything – and you won’t know which one until it does.
