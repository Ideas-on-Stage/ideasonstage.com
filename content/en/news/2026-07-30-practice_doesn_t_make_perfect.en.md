People often say practice makes perfect.

It doesn’t.

Practice only reinforces whatever you repeat.

If you keep rehearsing the same habits, you’ll get better at those habits – even the unhelpful ones.

Repetition without feedback makes those flaws harder to shake off.

That’s why great presenters don’t rely on practice alone. They record themselves. They ask for honest input. They look for the small adjustments that make the biggest difference.

Practice creates progress when it’s guided.

If you want to get better, practise with direction, not on autopilot.
